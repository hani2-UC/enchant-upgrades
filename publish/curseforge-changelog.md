Initial release for Minecraft 1.20.1 and Forge 47.4.10+ (47.x).

- Replace random enchanting-table rolls with selected, incremental enchantment upgrades.
- Add material and XP level costs for 37 non-curse vanilla enchantments.
- Preserve existing enchantments, item names, and durability.
- Enforce vanilla enchantment compatibility and level limits, plus bookshelf requirements.
- Support equipment, regular books, enchanted books, Shift-click transfers, and item return on close.
- Include Japanese and English interfaces and data-pack configurable recipes.

Validation: all eight Minecraft GameTests passed. A Japanese client test confirmed table opening and an upgrade through the actual client/server connection. Other mod combinations are untested.
